# Framework Supply Agreement - Computer Equipment (SYNTHETIC)

## §1 Parties
This agreement is made between Example Holding Company (the Buyer) and Najd Computer Supplies Co.,
VAT number 300123456700003 (the Supplier), for the supply of computer equipment during 2026.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## §2.1 Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay each correct and undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days of the invoice date.
Invoices must reference a valid purchase order issued by the Buyer.

## §2.2 Bank Details
Payments are made only to the Supplier's bank account registered in the Buyer's vendor master.
Any change of bank details must be requested in writing and verified by phone before it takes effect.

<!-- page: 9 -->
## §3.4 Late Delivery Penalty
For late delivery the Supplier pays a penalty of 0.5% of the order value per day of delay,
capped at 10% of the order value.

<!-- page: 15 -->
## §4.2 Delivery
Delivery to any Buyer site in the Kingdom is included in the unit prices at no additional cost.
The Supplier shall not invoice shipping, freight or delivery charges.
