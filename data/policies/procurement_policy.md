## §1 Segregation of Duties
Procurement staff may submit invoices but may not approve payments. Final payment approval is
reserved for the Chief Financial Officer.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## §3.1 Prior Authorisation
Any invoice, including maintenance invoices, exceeding SAR 10,000 must be covered by a purchase
order or written prior authorisation. Invoices above this amount without prior authorisation
shall not be paid until authorised.

## §3.2 Three-way Match
Goods invoices are matched against the purchase order and the goods receipt before payment.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## §4 Vendor Bank Changes
Changes to vendor bank details require written request and call-back verification.
Invoices showing unverified bank details are placed on security hold.
